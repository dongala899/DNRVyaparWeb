FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about DNR Vyapar

DNR stands for Digital Net Register. DNR Vyapar is a desktop GST billing and invoicing application built with Electron for Indian small businesses, freelancers, and service providers.

DNR Vyapar starts with a 15-day free trial on first launch. After the trial, a subscription is required. Choose between monthly or yearly plans — whichever suits your budget. There are two tiers: Standard (1 company) and Pro (2 companies). Yearly plans include a significant discount compared to monthly billing.

Yes. DNR Vyapar is a fully offline Electron desktop application. All data — invoices, quotations, customers, vendors, items, users — is stored locally on your computer. Internet is only needed to download the installer or check for updates.

Your data never leaves your computer. Unlike cloud-based billing solutions, there is no external server storing your invoices, customer details, or financial records. You have complete control. Regular backups to JSON files are recommended for extra safety.

Yes. Invoices support HSN/SAC codes, automatic GST calculation with CGST/SGST/IGST split, round-off, discount, shipping address, and due dates. Invoices can be previewed, printed on A4, exported to PDF, and shared via WhatsApp directly from the app.

Create quotations with auto-generated reference numbers, customer selection, line items, tax, and covering letter text. Once a customer accepts, convert any saved quotation into an invoice with one click — the customer and line items are copied into the invoice form automatically.

From invoice history, select an invoice and click "Record Payment" to log payments with date, amount, and mode (Cash, Bank Transfer, UPI, or Cheque). The dashboard shows an overdue invoice banner with aging breakdown. The AR Aging report groups overdue invoices into day buckets with totals.

DNR Vyapar includes 9 advanced reports: GST Returns (GSTR-1 style), Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, Customer Ledger, Vendor Ledger, Tax Summary, Sales Register, Purchase Register, and AR Aging. Each supports date range filters and Excel export.

Yes. Depending on your license plan, you can manage up to 2 companies within the same installation. Each company has its own invoices, customers, vendors, items, and reports. The Pro license supports 2 companies.

Customer POs let you record purchase orders received from customers with PO number, date, items, and status tracking. Organization POs let you manage your own purchase orders to vendors with item autocomplete, tax computation, and tax summary. Purchases let you record vendor invoices or orders with vendor selection, items, tax, and totals.

Click "Backup Data" to save all records to a single JSON file. You can also use Ctrl+B. Before restoring, take a fresh backup. Then click "Restore Data" and select your backup file. Recommended to back up at the end of each working day.

Yes. Each section in Manage Data (Customers, Vendors, Items) supports CSV import and export. Click "Template" to download a blank CSV with correct headers, fill in your data, then use "Import CSV" to upload.

Ctrl+S (Save), Ctrl+N (New), Ctrl+P (Print), Ctrl+B (Backup), Ctrl+H (History), Ctrl+F (Search), Ctrl+D (Dark mode), Alt+1-5 (Tabs), Escape (Close modal), Ctrl+? (Shortcuts help).

Yes. Press Ctrl+D or use the dark mode button to toggle between light and dark themes. Applies across all workspaces, modals, forms, reports, and dropdowns.

DNR Vyapar runs on Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit). Built with Electron 41.7.0, packaged as an NSIS installer. Minimum: 4 GB RAM, 100 MB disk space.

Take a backup on the current computer. Install DNR Vyapar on the new computer. Open the app, then use "Restore Data" to load your backup file. Verify all records are visible and correct.

Email us at dnrvyapar@gmail.com for support. If the app behaves unexpectedly, take a backup first if possible, then reach out with details of what you were doing when the issue occurred.
